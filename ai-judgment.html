AI and Academic Judgment

From Grading Output to Evaluating Thinking.

Generative AI fundamentally changes what a text represents. A well-written essay is no longer proof of a well-structured thought process. This gap requires a new approach to academic judgment.

If final output can be simulated, assessment has to move upstream. It must capture how ideas are formed, how claims are tested, and whether the student voice is authentic.

This is why we build infrastructure that makes the intellectual process visible.

  • Socrates validates the questioning process.
  • Diotima validates the provenance of the writing.
  • Isocrates validates the structural coherence.

By securing these dimensions, we ensure that the grade awarded at the end of the semester remains a meaningful measure of human capability, not just a measure of prompt engineering.
