Isocrates

Academic conformity and argumentative coherence

Isocrates is a device for analyzing the conformity of theses and long academic works to university canons, as well as the formal coherence of their argumentation.

A thesis is assessed within a precise institutional framework. Isocrates verifies that this framework is respected consistently throughout the document.

It systematically examines the way the work fits into expected academic frameworks — structuring of reasoning, articulation of arguments, and formal coherence of the whole — without assessing the disciplinary content itself.

This analysis allows for objectifying adherence to university standards and providing the jury or supervisor with a stable basis for exercising academic judgment.
