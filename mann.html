Mann Vergan

VP for Global Partnerships

Mann Vergan is an attorney qualified in New York, Massachusetts and England & Wales. He has extensive experience as a corporate lawyer and has worked for such firms as CMS Cameron McKenna, Clifford Chance, Mourant Ozannes and Harneys.

More recently he was a partner at Vistra, the global corporate services provider, where he advised funds, trusts, investment managers, multinationals, retailers, corporate and private clients.

He is also a consultant for End Single Use Plastics Now, a non-profit, grassroots advocacy organization on ending plastic pollution with an emphasis on Africa.
