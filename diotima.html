Diotima

Authorship & Authenticity Review

Diotima supports pre-grading authenticity review, helping institutions assess whether submitted work reflects the student's own reasoning process.

Diotima evaluates each submission across three complementary layers:

  • Stylistic and semantic: coherence of expression, consistency of voice, and language patterns
  • Cognitive: signs of progressive reasoning, revision, and refinement
  • Argumentative: logical dependence between premises and conclusions

This analysis helps estimate how much of the final work reflects original student thinking versus external generation or unsupported assistance.


When generated text can imitate human writing, authenticity checks become essential. Diotima helps review teams work from documented indicators rather than assumptions.

Role in the review process

  • Before grading: establish authorship and authenticity indicators
  • During grading: support decisions with stronger evidence
