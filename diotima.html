Diotima

AI usage and authorship analysis

What it produces

Diotima produces an argued report indicating where and how AI may have been used, with a confidence level for each finding.

Diotima is designed for submitted work where authorship and use of AI may affect the validity of the final decision.

The analysis is structured across:

  • stylistic signals
  • reasoning patterns
  • argumentative structure

What makes it different

Similarity detection is not enough.
Diotima identifies patterns consistent with AI-assisted writing.
Each finding is explained, located, and qualified.

Illustration: Authenticity analysis applied to a student paper.

Why it matters

When authorship is uncertain, decisions weaken.
Diotima turns suspicion into structured evidence.

What it costs not to have it

  • doubt remains
  • reviews are repeated
  • decisions rely on assumptions
