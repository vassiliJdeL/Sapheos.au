Diotima

Authenticity and provenance of reasoning

Diotima is a device for analyzing the authenticity of student work, intervening upstream of assessment to secure its conditions.

Diotima examines a work based on three complementary levels:

  • Stylistic and semantic: coherence of expression, continuity of voice, lexical stability
  • Cognitive: traces of progressive elaboration of reasoning, reformulations, adjustments
  • Argumentative: logical dependence between premises and conclusions

This analysis allows for appreciating the respective place of human intellectual activity and automated assistance in the submitted production.


In a context where automated productions can imitate the form of human reasoning, only an automated analysis device allows for rigorously qualifying its origin, so that assessment focuses on reasoning whose provenance is clearly established.

Role in the assessment chain

  • Upstream: establishment of authenticity and provenance of reasoning
  • Downstream: assessment based on intellectually defensible work
