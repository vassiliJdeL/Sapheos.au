System Security & NIS2

The security of academic integrity relies on the security of the systems that support it. Grades Education designs and operates its tools as critical academic infrastructure, integrating cybersecurity from the system design phase.

Our approach favors structural technical measures aimed at limiting attack surfaces and the potential impact of an incident.

Applied Security Principles

  • Server Security
    Servers hosting Grades Education services are configured according to strict security standards: access control, service partitioning, regular updates, and continuous monitoring. Hosting in France and limiting external dependencies contribute to reducing risks related to the technical chain.
  • Tool Security
    Tools are designed to limit privileges, restrict access to only necessary functionalities, and avoid any unnecessary exposure. The absence of persistent storage of personal data structurally reduces the potential consequences of a compromise.
  • Exchange Security
    Data exchanges between users, systems, and technical components are protected by adapted encryption mechanisms, guaranteeing the confidentiality and integrity of flows throughout the processing duration.
  • Risk Surface Reduction
    The automatic deletion of data after processing, combined with the absence of history and persistent databases, strongly limits the interest and impact of an attack targeting data.

Cybersecurity Skills and Culture

The technical and organizational choices implemented by Grades Education rely on internal cybersecurity skills, developed in demanding institutional and European contexts.

Team members are regularly involved in spaces for reflection, training, and awareness regarding cybersecurity and the intersecting issues of artificial intelligence and information system protection. This dynamic notably includes:

  • interventions and exchanges within academic and professional frameworks dedicated to cybersecurity and AI,
  • engagement in continuous cybersecurity training paths, to maintain a high level of requirement and skill updating.

Alignment with the NIS2 Directive

The measures implemented follow a logic of alignment with the requirements of the European NIS2 Directive, particularly regarding:

  • risk management,
  • service continuity and availability,
  • protection against cybersecurity incidents.

Security is not treated as an additional layer, but as an intrinsic property of the systems operated by Grades Education.
