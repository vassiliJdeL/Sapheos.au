Data Protection (GDPR)

Grades Education enforces a data protection policy based on restrictive technical choices, aiming to strictly limit exposure to personal data by principle.

Applied Principles

  • Hosting in France: All services are operated on servers located in France. No data is hosted or processed outside of this perimeter.
  • No Storage of Personal Data: Data transmitted during the use of our tools is not retained. It transits only for the strict time necessary for processing, then is automatically deleted.
  • No History Creation: Grades Education does not build any user database and does not retain any academic content beyond the execution of the requested service.
  • No Use for Training Purposes: No data processed by Grades Education is used for training, improving, or calibrating artificial intelligence models, whether internal or provided by third parties.
  • Priority to European Infrastructures: Tool and provider choices prioritize European solutions and infrastructures, provided they meet security and performance requirements.

Compliance by Design

Compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR – EU 2016/679) is based on a logic of privacy by design and strict data minimization, integrated from the very design of our systems.
