Our Conception of Assessment

Academic assessment is an act of judgment.

We believe that assessment exists to test reasoning, not to reward output.

It is grounded in the confrontation of ideas, the examination of coherence, and the responsibility of deciding whether a line of thought holds. Any system that substitutes performance for reasoning undermines the very purpose of academic evaluation.

In the traditions of Socrates, who tested understanding through questioning, and Aristotle, who grounded judgment in structure and criteria, the visibility of reasoning was taken for granted.

Under conditions shaped by generative AI, this assumption can no longer be safely maintained. When textual production becomes available without corresponding cognitive effort, the act of evaluation loses its footing and judgments become difficult to defend.

The challenge, therefore, is not adaptation to a technology, but the preservation of assessment as a defensible institutional act. Grades Education builds the infrastructure that allows judgment to remain possible under these conditions.
