Parmenides

Citation & Source Verification

What it produces

Parmenides verifies that cited sources exist and are correctly referenced.

Parmenides is used where cited sources matter to the validity of the assessment.

It identifies:

  • non-existent references
  • incorrect citations
  • unverifiable sources

What makes it different

Parmenides does not assess content.
It focuses only on the validity of sources.
Each reference is checked against verified sources.

Illustration: Cross-checking citations in a student submission against trusted sources.

Why it matters

Academic evaluation depends on verifiable sources.
Parmenides ensures the decision rests on real material.

What it costs not to have it

  • false references go undetected
  • synthetic bibliographies pass
  • decisions rely on unverifiable material
