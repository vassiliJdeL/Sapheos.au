Scholarly Integrity Defense

Academic discourse relies on shared, verifiable, and stable references. In a context marked by the proliferation of synthetic content, the reliability of citations and sources constitutes a central issue of scientific integrity.


Parmenides is designed as a mechanism for the defense of scholarly integrity. It aims to ensure that academic productions remain anchored in existing literature, rather than drifting towards self-referential chains or hallucinated citations.


By ensuring the stability and traceability of sources, Parmenides keeps academic work within the cumulative conversation of knowledge, an essential condition of any scientific approach.

Function

Parmenides performs a systematic cross-verification of citations and references present in academic works.

It identifies:

  • non-existent or incorrect references,
  • citations attributed to non-verifiable sources,
  • partially or entirely synthetic bibliographies.

The tool makes no disciplinary judgment on the content. It intervenes exclusively on the documentary and referential validity of the submitted work.

Operating Principle

Parmenides is based on strict Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), designed to prevent any dissociation between claim and source.


Each reference is confronted with a verified knowledge graph of academic literature. Any citation not finding an explicit match in this database is flagged as problematic.


This approach guarantees that generation or analysis remains subordinate to the effective existence of sources, and not to linguistic plausibility.

Institutional Benefits

Citation Verification

Detection and flagging of hallucinated, erroneous, or untraceable references.

Source Traceability

Direct linking of claims with identified primary sources.

Integrity Reporting

Production of a report attesting to the consistency and reliability of the bibliography.

Parmenides thus constitutes a documentary security tool, serving teachers, juries, and institutions anxious to preserve the scientific rank of their evaluations.
