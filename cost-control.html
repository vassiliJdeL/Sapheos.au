Cost control

Disjointed high-stakes evaluations silently drain institutional resources and inflate administrative budgets. Sapheos provides the architecture to eliminate these hidden costs:

  • Streamlined Faculty Workload: Reduce time lost to inconsistent grading standards and redundant committee meetings.
  • Eliminated Thesis Rework: Prevent endless revision loops between supervisors and students through clear, structured oversight.
  • Centralized Admin Coordination: Replace fragmented emails and manual tracking with a single, auditable workflow.
  • Predictable Economics: Transform unpredictable thesis supervision into a standardized, cost-efficient process.
