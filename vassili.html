Dr. Vassili Joannidès de Lautour

President & Founder

PhD, HDR, ENS, Agrégé

Holder of a PhD, Habilitation to Direct Research (HDR), École Normale Supérieure alumnus and Agrégé, he has over twenty years of experience in higher education and research.

He has held senior academic responsibilities, notably as Director of the Doctoral School at Grenoble École de Management, and has taught or collaborated with institutions such as Grenoble École de Management, Queensland University of Technology, and Columbia University.

A regular evaluator for national quality assurance bodies (including HCERES), his work focuses on academic assessment, scientific integrity, doctoral studies, and governance issues introduced by generative AI in higher education.
