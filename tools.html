Academic Infrastructure for Assessment

Designed for institutions that require reliable, fair assessment in the age of generative AI.

Integrated Assessment Infrastructure

Grades Education provides an integrated toolset that supports the full assessment workflow. Instead of replacing faculty judgment, it gives institutions stronger evidence and clearer decision support.

Socrates

Authentic Student Engagement

Supports active questioning before writing begins, so students show their reasoning, not just a polished final answer.

Diotima

Provenance & Authenticity Verification

Analyzes writing patterns and reasoning signals to help distinguish authentic student work from synthetic generation.

Isocrates

Argumentative Assessment

Evaluates thesis and long-form argument quality, with clear checks for structure, coherence, and academic standards.

Xenophon

Applied Competency Validation

Measures how well students apply academic knowledge to real-world scenarios, decisions, and trade-offs.

Parmenides

Scholarly Integrity Defense

Cross-checks citations and references to catch invented or unverified sources and protect research integrity.
