Integrated Assessment Infrastructure

Grades Education provides an integrated toolset for assessment operations. It supports institutional review processes with stronger documentation, clearer controls, and consistent decision support.

Socrates

Authentic Student Engagement

Documents structured questioning before writing begins, so review teams can evaluate reasoning process, not only final text quality.

Diotima

Authorship & Authenticity Review

Analyzes writing patterns and process indicators to support authenticity review and authorship confidence.

Isocrates

Structure & Standards Review

Evaluates capstone and long-form submissions for structure, coherence, and alignment with institutional writing standards.

Xenophon

Applied Competency Assessment

Assesses how well students apply program knowledge in scenario-based decisions and trade-off analysis.

Parmenides

Citation & Source Verification

Cross-checks citations and references to detect invented or unverified sources and strengthen documentation quality.
