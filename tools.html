These are not grading tools.

Grades Education tools ensure your highest-stakes academic decisions stand up to any scrutiny.

Euclid: Delivers transparent Assurance of Learning for high-stakes capstones, giving students equitable, objective validation of their culminating academic mastery.
Diotima: Fosters true intellectual ownership by untangling human authorship from AI assistance, protecting the integrity and long-term value of the student's authentic voice.
Parmenides: Elevates research rigor by systematically validating citations, training students to construct their arguments anchored exclusively in credible, verifiable evidence.
Socrates: Intercepts foundational errors early by actively scaffolding the research framing process, ensuring students begin writing with a robust, defensible thesis.
Aristotle: Bridges the gap between the classroom and the workforce by rigorously translating subjective internship and project experiences into concrete, career-ready professional competencies.

These tools are designed for the evaluations that define degrees: dissertations, capstones, internships, and competency-based reports.
