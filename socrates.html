Socrates

Structured questioning before dissertation, capstone, or research writing

What it produces

Socrates produces a structured sequence of questions that forces the student to define:

Socrates is used before writing begins, when the topic, question, and argument still need to be fixed.

  • the topic
  • the research question
  • the argument

before any writing begins.

What makes it different

Most tools generate answers.
Socrates does not generate content.
It forces the student to make their reasoning explicit.
The output is not text.
It is a clarified line of thinking.

Illustration: Guided questioning for developing a complex academic question.

Why it matters

When reasoning remains implicit, assessment relies on the final text alone.
Socrates makes the thinking visible before submission.

What it costs not to have it

  • topics remain unclear
  • research questions stay weak
  • supervision cycles repeat
