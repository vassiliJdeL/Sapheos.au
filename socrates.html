Socrates

Authentic Student Engagement

Socrates is an academic questioning device intervening upstream of assessment, designed to make student reasoning explicit before any assessed output.

Socrates organizes structured questioning sequences that lead the student to formulate, clarify, and justify their thought before drafting an assignment, a thesis, or any output submitted for assessment.


Reasoning is thus stabilized upstream, enabling the entire subsequent assessment process to rest on coherent and defensible intellectual foundations.

Demo: Questioning chatbot applied to the formulation of an academic research question.

Institutional Guarantee

Reasoning is made explicit before any written submission

Cognitive engagement is observable and traceable

Assessment no longer relies solely on the submitted text
