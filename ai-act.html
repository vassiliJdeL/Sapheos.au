Trusted AI & AI Act

The artificial intelligence devices developed by Grades Education are designed to meet the requirements of the European AI Act framework, particularly when uses fall within educational contexts likely to be qualified as "high risk".

Our approach is based on explicit design choices, aiming to guarantee institutional responsibility and the primacy of human judgment.

Applied Principles

  • Systematic Human Oversight: The analysis tools provided by Grades Education assist academic judgment without ever substituting for it. No critical academic decision (final grading, validation, sanction) is fully automated.
  • Transparency of Analysis Criteria: Evaluation logics, mobilized criteria, and analysis frameworks are documented and explicable. Grades Education refuses any approach based on opaque mechanisms or decision-making "black boxes".
  • Functional Limitation of Systems: Tools are voluntarily circumscribed to functions of analysis, structuring, and aid to evaluation. They produce neither autonomous normative verdicts nor enforceable decisions.
  • Robustness and Vigilance Against Bias: Systems are designed and tested to limit cognitive, cultural, or methodological biases likely to alter the equity of academic treatment.

Awareness of the AI Act Framework

Grades Education informs and raises awareness among its clients and partners regarding the AI Act requirements applicable to the uses of artificial intelligence in higher education, particularly when these uses fall into "high risk" categories.

This awareness-raising covers in particular:

  • obligations of human oversight,
  • institutional responsibility in the deployment of tools,
  • issues related to usage contexts and mobilized data.

Grades Education does not act as legal counsel, but contributes to an informed and responsible appropriation of the European regulatory framework by user institutions.
