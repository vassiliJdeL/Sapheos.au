Responsible AI Governance

Grades Education AI systems are designed for educational environments where accountability, explainability, and fairness are high-priority requirements.

Our approach uses explicit design controls to preserve institutional responsibility and keep human judgment at the center of decision-making.

Applied Principles

  • Human-in-the-Loop Oversight: Grades Education tools support institutional review teams. Final grading, progression decisions, and disciplinary outcomes remain human decisions.
  • Transparent Evaluation Criteria: The criteria and analytic methods used by our tools are documented and explainable. We avoid opaque, black-box decision logic.
  • Purpose-Limited System Design: Tools are scoped to analysis, structuring, and decision support. They do not issue binding academic determinations on their own.
  • Bias Monitoring and Mitigation: Systems are tested and monitored to reduce unfair outcomes tied to cognitive, cultural, or methodological bias.

Alignment with U.S. Guidance

Grades Education helps institutions implement responsible AI practices in line with U.S.-focused guidance frameworks, including NIST AI RMF-style controls and emerging federal and state expectations for educational AI use.

Implementation support focuses on:

  • human oversight requirements,
  • institutional accountability during rollout,
  • risk-aware data and context governance.

Grades Education does not provide legal advice, but supports informed, practical, and auditable AI governance for U.S. institutions.
