Euclid

Structure & Standards Review

Euclid reviews capstone and long-form submissions for structure, coherence, and alignment with institutional standards.

Each submission is evaluated within clear academic expectations. Euclid checks whether those expectations are met consistently across the full document.

It examines organization of reasoning, argument flow, and formal consistency, without replacing discipline-specific academic review.

This gives academic leadership, program directors, and review teams a stable evidence base for decisions.

Illustration: Structural and argumentative analysis of a long-form submission.

Institutional Value

Evaluation criteria mapped to explicit program standards

Structure and coherence assessed without overriding discipline-level review
