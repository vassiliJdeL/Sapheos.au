Why Philosophers?

Grades Education tools are named after philosophers because each tool represents a non-negotiable function of academic judgment, not a gadget.

Good assessment means evaluating reasoning: making it visible, checking where it comes from, testing its structure, and verifying its sources.

  • Socrates: makes reasoning explicit
  • Diotima: establishes authorship and authenticity
  • Euclid: validates structure and standards
  • Aristotle: assesses applied judgment
  • Parmenides: verifies references

Generative AI did not change these requirements.
It made their absence visible.
