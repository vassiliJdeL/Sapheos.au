Why Philosophers?

Grades Education tools are named after philosophers because each one represents a core function of academic judgment, not a gadget.

Good assessment means evaluating reasoning: making it visible, checking where it comes from, testing its structure, and verifying its sources.

  • Socrates: making reasoning explicit
  • Diotima: establishing its authenticity
  • Isocrates: structuring argument
  • Xenophon: translating reasoning into action
  • Parmenides: validating references

Generative AI did not create these requirements.
It made them impossible to ignore.
